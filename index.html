R. Block Nation — Progress in Partnership

R. Block Nation (R. Block Share Holdings LLC) appreciates the continued collaboration and trust of its compliance partners and regulatory counterparts.
Operating today under the supervision of a FINRA-regulated Broker-Dealer relationship with Andes Capital, the platform has advanced from concept to an integrated, BD-supervised environment designed for transparency, equity, and sustainable expansion.

Our Mission

Our mission is to expand compliant access to capital for small businesses and investors across diverse communities.
Guided by broker-dealer supervision, R. Block Nation continues to invest in education, transparency, and outreach — dedicating meaningful marketing and learning resources to under-served founders and retail investor markets.

Current Focus

R. Block Nation is in an active build phase — strengthening integrations between North Capital’s TransactAPI, on-chain smart contracts, and cap table/transfer agency systems. Each workflow aligns with BD compliance standards and prepares the foundation for Reg A+ expansion.

Key Milestones

  • ✅ BD Supervision (Andes Capital) — relationship active and verified.
  • ✅ North Capital Sandbox Integration — credentials confirmed; API endpoints under test.
  • ✅ Reg CF Token Deployed — View on Sepolia.
  • ✅ Revenue-Share Token (Reg A+ Ready) — BFTKN Verified on Sepolia.

Looking Ahead

R. Block Nation’s next phase focuses on: - Completing Pre-Live Certification for API workflows under BD oversight.
- Expanding issuer onboarding and investor education pathways.
- Transitioning from the current Reg CF framework toward Reg A+ raise readiness.

This project remains a work in progress — deliberately paced to ensure that every advancement supports compliance, education, and access for all participants.
